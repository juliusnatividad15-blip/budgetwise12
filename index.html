Nothing here started new.

Reclaimed Cycles began on a marketplace listing, not a shop floor — a single loose derailleur, cleaned up and sold to a neighbor who needed it more than the drawer did. It's grown into a small bench of regulars who'd rather rebuild a part than replace it.

Every listing below started life on someone else's bike. We inspect it, rate its condition honestly, and price it like it still has work to do — because it does.

How a part gets a second ride

Same four passes on every listing, in this order, no shortcuts.

  1. 01

    Strip & inspect

    Fully disassembled, every surface checked for cracks, corrosion or wear past spec.

  2. 02

    Clean & decontaminate

    Degreased, ultrasonic-bathed where needed, rust neutralized on steel parts.

  3. 03

    Rebuild & true

    Bearings, springs and pivots replaced or repacked; wheels trued to within 0.5mm.

  4. 04

    Tag & list

    Condition scored out of 10 and written on the tag — the same number you'll see in the listing.

Before you message us

How honest is the condition rating, really?

It's the same number our own bench notes use. An 8/10 has cosmetic wear only; anything with a functional issue gets flagged in the listing, not buried in the number.

Can I request a part be rebuilt to spec?

Yes — message us the part and your torque or fit requirements before we list it, and we'll rebuild to order where the part allows it.

Do you ship, or pickup only?

Local meetups are preferred for wheels and frames so you can inspect true and alignment yourself; smaller drivetrain parts can ship.

Where do parts come from?

Trade-ins, donated drawers, and bikes retired for parts — never anything reported stolen, and never a part we haven't opened up ourselves.
